To many of the Middle Eastern nations there is of course no need for either recipes or introductions for Bulgor has been their main stay of diet for many Centuries. We have two purposes in presenting this booklet to the Canadian public. Our first objective is to help Canadian farmers and the Government in their efforts to reduce the country's wheat surplus, this we hope to do by converting a part of our surplus wheat into Bulgor and offering it to the public with the help of this recipe booklet. Secondly; because there is a generation of Canadians who like to venture beyond the beaten path of known taste and try the eating habits of other nations, we wish to share our way of cooking some recipes with these Canadians.

These recipes are not only exotic and different but are beneficial to health and simple to prepare. There are no ingredients which are difficult to find. Anyone who likes to cook can always better a recipe that has been passed on to her. So, some of the seasonings mentioned may be omitted or used in lesser quantities according to individual tastes.

In some of the recipes included here, cooked Bulgor is listed as an ingredient. The simple recipe below can be used to make cooked Bulgor from the dry Bulgor Wheat. Usually cooking is the part of the preparation of the soup, entrée or other dish served warm.

We hope you will use Bulgor Wheat as often as possible since Bulgor is versatile in cookery and is usable in every course and every meal of the day. Bulgor is one of the few natural foods that can be used in all types of dishes.

SHEHIRIAN BULGOR CO.

We thank the U.S. Dept. of Agriculture for supplying some of the recipes in this booklet.
