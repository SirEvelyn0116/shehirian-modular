كعكة البرغل بالموز

كعكة على شكل رغيف تحتوي على برغل مطبوخ لإضافة القوام والنكهة.

الفئة: حلوى   |   المطبخ: مخبوز

وقت التحضير: 20 دقيقة   |   وقت الطهي: 1 ساعة 15 دقيقة   |   الإجمالي: 1 ساعة 35 دقيقة

المكونات

  • 1/3 cup Bulgor Wheat
  • 1-1/3 cups water (for Bulgor)
  • 2 cups sifted all-purpose flour
  • 3 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • 3/4 cup mashed banana
  • 1 teaspoon lemon juice
  • 1/4 cup milk

التحضير

  1. Combine Bulgor and water; cover and simmer about 20 minutes until most water is absorbed.
  2. Sift together flour, baking powder, and salt.
  3. Cream butter and sugar until light and fluffy; add eggs and beat until thick and creamy.
  4. Blend cooked Bulgor, vanilla, mashed banana, and lemon juice into the creamed mixture; add flour mixture alternately with milk.
  5. Turn into greased loaf pan and bake at 350°F about 1¼ hours.
