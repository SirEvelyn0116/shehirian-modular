Pilaf Almondine

A simple bulgur pilaf garnished with toasted almonds and poppy seeds.

Catégorie: Accompagnement   |   Cuisine: Internationale

Temps de préparation: 5 minutes   |   Temps de cuisson: 25 minutes   |   Total: 35 minutes

Ingrédients

  • 1 cup Bulgor Wheat
  • 2 cups water
  • 1/2 cup butter or margarine
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • Chopped toasted almonds (for garnish)
  • Poppy seeds (for garnish)

Instructions

  1. Combine Bulgor, salt, and water in a saucepan.
  2. Bring to boil, add butter or margarine, cover, reduce heat and simmer until water is absorbed (15 to 20 minutes).
  3. Let it stand 10 minutes.
  4. Sprinkle each serving lightly with poppy seeds and 1 to 2 teaspoons chopped toasted almonds.
