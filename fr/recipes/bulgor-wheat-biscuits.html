Bulgor Wheat Biscuits

Tender biscuits made with cooked bulgur mixed into the dough.

Catégorie: Accompagnement   |   Cuisine: Cuit au four

Temps de préparation: 15 minutes   |   Temps de cuisson: 50 minutes   |   Total: 65 minutes

Ingrédients

  • 1 cup water
  • 1/4 cup Bulgor wheat
  • 2 cups sifted flour
  • 3-1/2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 cup shortening
  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 3/4 cup cooked Bulgor

Instructions

  1. Combine water and Bulgor wheat; cover and simmer about 20 minutes until most water is absorbed.
  2. Sift together flour, baking powder, and salt; cut in shortening.
  3. Stir in milk and cooked Bulgor, knead lightly, pat or roll out 1/2-inch thick and cut into biscuits.
  4. Bake at 400°F until lightly browned, about 25 to 30 minutes.
