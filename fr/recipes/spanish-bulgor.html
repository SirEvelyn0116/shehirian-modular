Spanish Bulgor

A hearty skillet bulgur with bacon, peppers, tomatoes and seasonings.

Catégorie: Accompagnement   |   Cuisine: Inspiré espagnol

Temps de préparation: 10 minutes   |   Temps de cuisson: 25 minutes   |   Total: 35 minutes

Ingrédients

  • 4 slices bacon cut in 1/2-inch strips
  • 1/2 to 3/4 cup chopped onion
  • 1/2 cup chopped celery
  • 1/2 cup chopped green bell pepper
  • 1 clove garlic, finely minced
  • 1 can (10 oz.) tomatoes
  • 2 cups water
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon seasoned salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon pepper
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 1 teaspoon Worcestershire Sauce
  • 1 cup Bulgor Wheat

Instructions

  1. Sauté bacon strips in a heavy skillet until they begin to brown.
  2. Add onion, celery, green bell pepper, and garlic and cook until onion is straw-coloured.
  3. Blend in the tomatoes, water, seasonings, and Bulgor Wheat.
  4. Cover and simmer over low heat until most of liquid is absorbed, 20 to 25 minutes.
