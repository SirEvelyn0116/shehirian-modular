Fish and Bulgur Pilaf

A bulgur pilaf flavored with sautéed vegetables and chunks of salmon.

Category: Main Dish   |   Cuisine: International

Prep Time: 10 minutes   |   Cook Time: 30 minutes   |   Total: 40 minutes

Ingredients

  • 3 tablespoons butter or margarine
  • 1 pound fresh salmon, cubed
  • 1/2 cup chopped onion
  • 1/2 cup chopped celery
  • 1 cup bulgur wheat
  • 2 cups water
  • 1-2 tablespoons lemon juice
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/8 teaspoon fine herbs
  • 1/8 teaspoon thyme

Instructions

  1. Heat butter in skillet, add onion and celery and sauté until translucent.
  2. Add salmon and cook briefly, then add bulgur and water.
  3. Cover and simmer 25-30 minutes until bulgur is tender. Season with lemon juice and salt.
