Bulgur Dutch Cucumber Salad

A creamy and refreshing salad with bulgur, cucumbers, radishes, and a tangy sour cream dressing.

Category: Salad   |   Cuisine: Dutch

Prep Time: 15 minutes   |   Cook Time: 15 minutes   |   Total: 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cooked bulgur, cooled
  • 2 cups sliced cucumber
  • 1/2 cup sliced radishes
  • 1/2 cup sour cream
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp white vinegar
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Fresh dill or chives for garnish

Instructions

  1. Prepare bulgur and cool.
  2. Combine bulgur, cucumber slices and radishes in a bowl.
  3. Whisk together sour cream, mayonnaise, vinegar, salt and pepper; fold into salad.
  4. Garnish with dill or chives and chill before serving.
