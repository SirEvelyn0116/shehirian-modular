Bulgur Meat Loaf

A meatloaf enriched with bulgur wheat and baked until browned.

Category: Main Dish   |   Cuisine: International

Prep Time: 15 minutes   |   Cook Time: 1 hr   |   Total: 1 hr 15 min

Ingredients

  • 2-1/2 cups cooked bulgur
  • 1 egg
  • 1-1/4 cups milk
  • 2 teaspoons salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon pepper
  • 1/4 to 1/2 cup chopped onion
  • 1/4 cup ketchup
  • 2 pounds ground beef

Instructions

  1. Blend all ingredients together.
  2. Turn into loaf pan and bake at 325°F (160°C) about 1 hour or until browned.
  3. Optionally spread a thin layer of ketchup on top before baking.
