Scalloped Bulgur with Wieners

A creamy baked bulgur dish topped with wieners.

Category: Casserole   |   Cuisine: Comfort Food

Prep Time: 10 minutes   |   Cook Time: 50 minutes   |   Total: 1 hr

Ingredients

  • 2-1/2 cups cooked bulgur
  • 1 egg slightly beaten
  • 2 tbsp chopped onion
  • 1 can condensed cream of mushroom soup
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 5-6 wieners

Instructions

  1. Blend bulgur, egg, onion, condensed soup, salt and milk in shallow baking dish.
  2. Bake at 350°F (175°C) until partially set (~30 minutes).
  3. Top with wieners, brush with margarine and continue baking 15–20 minutes until browned.
