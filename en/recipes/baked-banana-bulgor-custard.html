Baked Banana Bulgor Custard

A baked custard-style dessert made with cooked bulgur and mashed banana.

Category: Dessert   |   Cuisine: Baked

Prep Time: 10 minutes   |   Cook Time: 45 minutes   |   Total: 55 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1-1/2 cups cooked Bulgor
  • 1/2 cup mashed banana
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1 teaspoon grated lemon rind
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • 1/3 cup sugar
  • 1/8 teaspoon salt
  • 2 cups milk
  • 2 eggs, slightly beaten
  • 1 tablespoon melted butter

Instructions

  1. Blend all ingredients in a baking dish.
  2. Sprinkle lightly with cinnamon.
  3. Bake in moderate oven for 40 to 45 minutes or until set.
