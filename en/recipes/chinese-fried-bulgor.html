Chinese Fried Bulgur

A quick fried bulgur with soy sauce and vegetables, served as a side or main.

Category: Main/Side   |   Cuisine: Chinese-Inspired

Prep Time: 5 minutes   |   Cook Time: 15 minutes   |   Total: 20 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 3/4 cup finely minced onion
  • 1/2 cup finely minced green bell pepper
  • 1/2 cup finely minced celery
  • 2-1/2 cups cooked bulgur
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce

Instructions

  1. Heat oil in skillet, sauté onion, bell pepper and celery until translucent.
  2. Stir in cooked bulgur and soy sauce; cover and cook over low heat 15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
