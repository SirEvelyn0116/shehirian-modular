Bulgur Cheese Casserole

A simple baked casserole mixing cooked bulgur with cheese and tomatoes.

Category: Casserole   |   Cuisine: International

Prep Time: 10 minutes   |   Cook Time: 30 minutes   |   Total: 40 minutes

Ingredients

  • 3 cups cooked bulgur
  • 1 15-oz can tomatoes
  • 1/3 cup sliced mushrooms
  • 1/2 lb pasteurized process cheese, sliced
  • 2 tbsp chopped green pepper
  • 2 tbsp chopped onion
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Blend cooked bulgur, mushrooms, green peppers, onion, salt and pepper.
  2. Place half of the mixture in a shallow casserole, cover with half the cheese slices.
  3. Add the rest of the bulgur mixture, arrange remaining cheese and bake at 350°F (175°C) for 30 minutes.
