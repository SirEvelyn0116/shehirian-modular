Bulgor Apricot Delight with Eggnog Sauce

A chilled apricot-flavored bulgur dessert topped with a rich eggnog-style sauce.

Category: Dessert   |   Cuisine: International

Prep Time: 10 minutes   |   Cook Time: 15 minutes   |   Total: 25 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup apricot nectar (or juice)
  • 1-1/2 cups water
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup Bulgor wheat
  • 3 tablespoons sugar (for Bulgor)
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • Apricot halves (for garnish)
  • Eggnog Sauce: 1 egg, 1/2 cup sugar, 1 teaspoon vanilla, 2 tablespoons sherry or rum (optional), 1/2 cup heavy cream, whipped

Instructions

  1. Mix apricot nectar, water, salt, and Bulgor in saucepan. Cover and simmer (do not boil) over low heat until all juice is absorbed; stir several times.
  2. Stir in 3 tablespoons sugar, cover and continue cooking over low heat for 5 minutes; chill the mixture and stir in lemon juice.
  3. Prepare Eggnog Sauce by beating the egg, gradually adding sugar and vanilla, add sherry/rum if using, then fold in whipped cream. Top each serving with Eggnog Sauce and half an apricot.
