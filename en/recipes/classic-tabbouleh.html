Classic Tabbouleh

A refreshing salad with parsley, mint, tomatoes, and fine bulgur.

Category: Salad   |   Cuisine: Middle Eastern

Prep Time: 20 minutes   |   Cook Time: 5 minutes   |   Total: 25 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup fine bulgur wheat
  • 2 cups finely chopped parsley
  • 1/2 cup finely chopped mint
  • 3 medium tomatoes, diced
  • 1/4 cup finely chopped onion
  • 1/4 cup fresh lemon juice
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Rinse bulgur wheat and soak in cold water for 5 minutes, then drain well.
  2. In a large bowl, combine parsley, mint, tomatoes, and onion.
  3. Add the drained bulgur wheat and mix well.
  4. In a small bowl, whisk together lemon juice, olive oil, salt, and pepper.
  5. Pour dressing over the salad and toss to combine.
  6. Refrigerate for at least 1 hour before serving to allow flavors to meld.
