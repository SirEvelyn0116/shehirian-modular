India Chicken Curry Pilaf

A creamy curried chicken pilaf with bulgur and apples.

Category: Main Dish   |   Cuisine: Indian-Inspired

Prep Time: 15 minutes   |   Cook Time: 25 minutes   |   Total: 40 minutes

Ingredients

  • Butter or chicken fat, chopped onion, tart apple, celery, curry powder, ginger, flour, salt, pepper, chicken stock, cream or milk, cooked diced chicken, bulgur

Instructions

  1. Sauté onion, apple and celery in butter; add spices and flour then stir in stock and cream until thickened.
  2. Add cooked chicken and bulgur, cover and simmer over low heat 25 minutes.
