India Lamb Pilaf

A spiced lamb pilaf with curry and apples, using bulgur.

Category: Main Dish   |   Cuisine: Indian-Inspired

Prep Time: 15 minutes   |   Cook Time: 40 minutes   |   Total: 55 minutes

Ingredients

  • Oil, cooked lamb cubes, chopped onion, tart apple, garlic, celery seed, curry powder, ginger, salt, pepper, Tabasco, tomato purée, consommé or water, 1 cup bulgur

Instructions

  1. Sauté lamb with onion, apple and garlic; brown lightly.
  2. Blend in remaining ingredients, bring to boil then simmer about 40 minutes until thickened.
