Boston Baked Bulgur

A sweet-and-savory baked bulgur dish finished with bacon or salt pork.

Category: Casserole   |   Cuisine: American

Prep Time: 10 minutes   |   Cook Time: 1 hr 25 min   |   Total: 1 hr 35 min

Ingredients

  • 2-1/2 cups cooked bulgur wheat
  • 2/3 cup condensed tomato soup
  • 3 tablespoons molasses
  • 3/4 cup water or consommé
  • 3 tablespoons brown sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon dry mustard
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 to 2 tablespoons minced onion
  • 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 ounces salt pork or bacon

Instructions

  1. Blend all ingredients except salt pork or bacon in a casserole.
  2. Cut salt pork or bacon into strips and press into surface of bulgur mixture.
  3. Cover and bake at 325°F (160°C) for 45 minutes; remove cover and continue baking about 40 minutes.
