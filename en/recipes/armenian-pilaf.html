Armenian Pilaf

A light pilaf with vermicelli and bulgur cooked in chicken stock.

Category: Side Dish   |   Cuisine: Armenian

Prep Time: 5 minutes   |   Cook Time: 20 minutes   |   Total: 25 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp butter or margarine
  • 3 tbsp chopped onion
  • 1/2 cup vermicelli or noodles
  • 1 cup bulgur wheat
  • 2 cups chicken stock
  • 3/4 tsp salt
  • 1/8 tsp pepper

Instructions

  1. Heat butter, sauté onion and vermicelli until lightly browned.
  2. Add bulgur, chicken stock, salt and pepper; cover and simmer 20 minutes until liquid absorbed.
  3. Let stand 15-20 minutes before serving.
