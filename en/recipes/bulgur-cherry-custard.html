Bulgur Cherry Custard

A layered bulgur dessert with a cherry compote and baked custard.

Category: Dessert   |   Cuisine: International

Prep Time: 20 minutes   |   Cook Time: 1 hr   |   Total: 1 hr 20 min

Ingredients

  • 2 cups hot cooked bulgur
  • 2 tablespoons butter or margarine
  • 1/4 cup brown sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1-1/2 tablespoons cornstarch
  • 1/4 cup sugar
  • Juice from 1 can (1 lb.) red sour pitted cherries
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • Drained cherries
  • 2 eggs, slightly beaten
  • 1 cup milk
  • 3 tablespoons sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla

Instructions

  1. Prepare the bulgur base by combining hot cooked bulgur, butter, brown sugar and cinnamon in a baking dish.
  2. In a saucepan, mix cornstarch and sugar, stir in drained cherry juice and cook until thickened. Stir in lemon juice and layer drained cherries over the bulgur.
  3. Whisk eggs, milk, sugar and vanilla; pour over the cherry layer and bake at 350°F (175°C) for 1 hour or until custard is set.
