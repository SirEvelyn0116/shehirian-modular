Bulgor Wheat Shortcake

A shortcake-style dessert or accompaniment using cooked bulgur in the dough.

Category: Side Dish   |   Cuisine: Baked

Prep Time: 15 minutes   |   Cook Time: 50 minutes   |   Total: 65 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup water
  • 1/4 cup Bulgor wheat
  • 2 cups sifted flour
  • 3-1/2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 3/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/3 cup sugar
  • 1/4 cup shortening
  • 3/4 cup milk

Instructions

  1. Combine water and Bulgor wheat; cover and simmer about 20 minutes until most water is absorbed.
  2. Sift together flour, baking powder, salt, and sugar.
  3. Cut in shortening, stir in cooked Bulgor and milk, pat into greased 8-inch cake pan.
  4. Brush surface lightly with milk, sprinkle with sugar and bake at 400°F about 30 minutes. Top with whipped cream and fruit.
