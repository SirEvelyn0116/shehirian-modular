White Bread with Bulgor

A soft white loaf made by incorporating cooked bulgur into the dough.

Category: Other   |   Cuisine: Baked

Prep Time: 30 minutes   |   Cook Time: 35 minutes   |   Total: 1 hr 5 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup water
  • 1/4 cup Bulgor wheat
  • 1/8 teaspoon salt (for Bulgor mixture)
  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 1 package (1/4 oz.) activated dry yeast
  • 1/2 cup lukewarm water
  • 4-1/4 cups all-purpose flour (reserve 1/4 cup for dusting)
  • 2 teaspoons salt (for flour mixture)
  • 3 tablespoons sugar
  • 3 tablespoons shortening or oil

Instructions

  1. Combine water, 1/4 cup Bulgor wheat, and 1/8 teaspoon salt. Cover and bring to simmer; simmer about 20 minutes or until most of water is absorbed. Cool to lukewarm.
  2. Dissolve yeast in 1/2 cup lukewarm water. Stir milk into the cooled Bulgor mixture.
  3. Sift together flour, salt, and sugar (reserving 1/4 cup flour). Add yeast mixture and cooked Bulgor to the flour mixture; add shortening or oil and mix thoroughly.
  4. Knead about 10 minutes or until dough is smooth; place in warm greased bowl, cover and let rise until doubled (1.5–2 hours).
  5. Punch down, divide into 2 loaves, shape and place into greased loaf pans; let rise until doubled and bake at 400°F (200°C) for 35–40 minutes.
