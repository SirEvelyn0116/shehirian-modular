Chocolate Bulgur Bavarian

A chilled chocolate and bulgur dessert set with gelatin and folded cream.

Category: Dessert   |   Cuisine: International

Prep Time: 30 minutes   |   Cook Time: 0 minutes   |   Total: 2 hr 30 min

Ingredients

  • 1 package gelatin
  • 2 tablespoons water
  • 2-1/2 tablespoons cocoa
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1-1/2 cups milk
  • 1-1/2 cups cooked bulgur
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream, whipped

Instructions

  1. Dissolve gelatin in water and heat with cocoa, sugar and milk until dissolved (do not boil).
  2. Stir in cooked bulgur and vanilla, chill until thick but not firm.
  3. Fold in whipped cream, turn into a greased mould and chill until firm.
