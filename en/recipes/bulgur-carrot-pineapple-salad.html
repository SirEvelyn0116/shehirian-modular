Bulgur Carrot Pineapple Salad

A sweet and savory salad combining bulgur wheat with shredded carrots and tropical pineapple.

Category: Salad   |   Cuisine: American

Prep Time: 10 minutes   |   Cook Time: 15 minutes   |   Total: 25 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked and cooled bulgur wheat
  • 2 cups shredded carrots
  • 1 cup pineapple chunks (fresh or canned)
  • 1/4 cup mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp sugar (optional)
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Cook bulgur according to package, cool to room temperature.
  2. In a large bowl combine bulgur, shredded carrots and pineapple.
  3. Whisk together mayonnaise, lemon juice, sugar, salt and pepper; fold into salad.
  4. Chill before serving.
