Bulgur Salmon Loaf

A salmon loaf combined with bulgur and baked until set.

Category: Main Dish   |   Cuisine: International

Prep Time: 15 minutes   |   Cook Time: 45 minutes   |   Total: 1 hr

Ingredients

  • 2-1/2 cups cooked bulgur
  • 1 can (16 oz.) salmon, drained
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup liquid (salmon liquid plus milk)
  • 1/4 cup chopped onion
  • 2 tablespoons lemon juice
  • 2 tablespoons pickle relish
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon pepper

Instructions

  1. Blend all ingredients and turn into a loaf pan.
  2. Bake in moderate oven, 350°F (175°C), for 45 minutes until set.
