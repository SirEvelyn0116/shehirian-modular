Persian Pilaf

A traditional Persian-style pilaf with lamb and bulgur.

Category: Main Dish   |   Cuisine: Persian

Prep Time: 20 minutes   |   Cook Time: 1 hr   |   Total: 1 hr 20 min

Ingredients

  • Shoulder of lamb, cubed
  • Chopped onion
  • Chopped tomatoes
  • 2 cups bulgur
  • Butter
  • Broth or water
  • Salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Braise lamb cubes until well browned; add onions and tomatoes and cook 15 minutes.
  2. Cook bulgur briefly in boiling broth, drain and place over meat; put butter in center and pour broth around edges.
  3. Bake in moderate oven about 1 hour; turn onto platter and serve hot unmixed.
