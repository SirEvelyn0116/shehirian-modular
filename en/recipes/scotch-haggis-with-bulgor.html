Scotch Haggis with Bulgur

A traditional haggis-style pudding mixed with bulgur.

Category: Pudding   |   Cuisine: Scottish

Prep Time: 30 minutes   |   Cook Time: 3 hr   |   Total: 3 hr 30 min

Ingredients

  • 3 lamb hearts
  • 1 lb liver
  • 1/2 lb minced suet
  • 1/2 cup chopped onions
  • 1 cup bulgur wheat
  • 3 tsp salt
  • 2 cups meat broth or bouillon

Instructions

  1. Simmer lamb hearts and liver in water 1.5 hours; cool and grind.
  2. Add suet, onions, bulgur, salt and broth; mix well and steam in covered pudding mold 3 hours.
