Bulgur Garden Salad

A versatile garden salad with bulgur wheat, fresh vegetables, and hard-boiled eggs — perfect as a side or main dish.

Category: Salad   |   Cuisine: American

Prep Time: 15 minutes   |   Cook Time: 15 minutes   |   Total: 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked bulgur wheat
  • 1 cup mixed chopped vegetables (tomato, cucumber, bell pepper)
  • 2 hard-boiled eggs, sliced
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Fresh parsley for garnish

Instructions

  1. Cook bulgur and allow to cool.
  2. Combine chopped vegetables and sliced eggs in a bowl.
  3. Add bulgur and toss gently.
  4. Whisk olive oil, lemon juice, salt and pepper; pour over salad and toss to coat.
  5. Chill briefly before serving.
