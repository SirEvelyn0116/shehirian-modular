Hearty Bulgur Pilaf

Coarse bulgur simmered with vegetables and spices for a comforting meal.

Category: Main Dish   |   Cuisine: Middle Eastern

Prep Time: 10 minutes   |   Cook Time: 25 minutes   |   Total: 35 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups coarse bulgur wheat
  • 2 tbsp butter or olive oil
  • 1 medium onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 1 carrot, diced
  • 3 cups vegetable broth
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1/2 tsp paprika
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Fresh herbs for garnish

Instructions

  1. In a large pot, heat butter or oil over medium heat.
  2. Add onion and garlic, sauté until fragrant and translucent.
  3. Add bell pepper and carrot, cook for 5 minutes until softened.
  4. Stir in bulgur wheat, cumin, and paprika, coating the grains with oil.
  5. Pour in vegetable broth, bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low.
  6. Cover and simmer for 15-20 minutes until bulgur is tender and liquid is absorbed.
  7. Remove from heat, let stand covered for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork.
  8. Season with salt and pepper, garnish with fresh herbs, and serve.
