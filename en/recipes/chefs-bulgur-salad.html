Chef's Bulgur Salad

A hearty main course salad featuring bulgur wheat, fresh vegetables, and your choice of cold meats and cheese.

Category: Main Course   |   Cuisine: American

Prep Time: 20 minutes   |   Cook Time: 15 minutes   |   Total: 35 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked bulgur wheat
  • 2 cups mixed salad greens
  • 1 cup diced tomatoes
  • 1 cup diced cucumber
  • 1 cup cold meats, sliced (optional)
  • 1 cup cubed cheese (optional)
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Prepare bulgur and let cool.
  2. In a large bowl combine bulgur, salad greens, tomatoes and cucumber.
  3. Add cold meats and cheese if using.
  4. Whisk olive oil, lemon juice, salt and pepper; toss with salad.
  5. Serve chilled or at room temperature.
