Cole Slaw with Bulgur

A hearty twist on classic coleslaw with the addition of nutritious bulgur wheat.

Category: Salad   |   Cuisine: American

Prep Time: 15 minutes   |   Cook Time: 10 minutes   |   Total: 25 minutes

Ingredients

  • 4 cups shredded cabbage
  • 1 cup shredded carrots
  • 1 cup cooked and cooled bulgur wheat
  • 1/2 cup mayonnaise
  • 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Cook bulgur according to package and cool.
  2. In a large bowl combine shredded cabbage and carrots.
  3. Add cooled bulgur.
  4. Whisk together mayonnaise, vinegar, sugar, salt and pepper; toss with vegetables.
  5. Chill before serving.
