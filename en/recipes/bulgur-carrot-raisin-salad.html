Bulgur Carrot Raisin Salad

A variation with raisins and French dressing for a delightful sweet and tangy flavor.

Category: Salad   |   Cuisine: American

Prep Time: 10 minutes   |   Cook Time: 15 minutes   |   Total: 25 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked bulgur wheat
  • 1 1/2 cups shredded carrots
  • 1/2 cup raisins
  • 1/2 cup French dressing (or vinaigrette)
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Combine cooked bulgur, shredded carrots, and raisins in a bowl.
  2. Add French dressing and toss to coat.
  3. Chill before serving.
