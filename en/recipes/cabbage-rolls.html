Cabbage Rolls

Cabbage leaves stuffed with a bulgur and lamb filling, simmered until tender.

Category: Main Dish   |   Cuisine: Middle Eastern

Prep Time: 30 minutes   |   Cook Time: 1 hr   |   Total: 1 hr 30 min

Ingredients

  • Cabbage leaves
  • 1-1/2 cups ground lamb
  • 2 small onions, chopped fine
  • 1-1/2 cups bulgur
  • 3 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 2 tablespoons chopped parsley
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried mint
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 3 cups meat broth or water
  • Juice of 1/2 lemon

Instructions

  1. Soften cabbage leaves in boiling water and trim ribs.
  2. Mix bulgur with lamb, onions, tomato paste, parsley, mint, salt and pepper.
  3. Place filling on cabbage leaves, roll and arrange in pan; add broth and lemon juice.
  4. Cover and cook or bake at moderate heat for 1 hour.
