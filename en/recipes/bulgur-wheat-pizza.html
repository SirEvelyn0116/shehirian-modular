Bulgur Wheat Pizza

A pizza variation using bulgur in the dough and a hearty meat-and-vegetable sauce.

Category: Main Dish   |   Cuisine: International

Prep Time: 1 hr 15 min   |   Cook Time: 40 minutes   |   Total: 1 hr 55 min

Ingredients

  • Pizza Sauce: oil, green bell pepper, onion, mushrooms, garlic, Italian sausage, lean ground beef, tomato purée, tomato sauce, water, Parmesan, bay leaf, pepper, salt, oregano
  • Pizza Dough: 1/2 cup bulgur, 1-3/4 cups water, active dry yeast, flour, salt, oil
  • Assembly: slices of mozzarella, grated Parmesan

Instructions

  1. Prepare the sauce: sauté vegetables and meats, add tomato purée, sauce, water, cheese and seasonings; simmer 90 minutes total.
  2. Prepare the dough: cook bulgur in water until absorbed, cool; dissolve yeast and mix with flour, bulgur, oil; let rise, divide, roll to 12-inch rounds.
  3. Assemble with mozzarella and sauce and bake at 375°F (190°C) for 35–40 minutes.
