Old-Fashioned Bulgur-Raisin Pudding

A baked bulgur pudding studded with raisins.

Category: Dessert   |   Cuisine: International

Prep Time: 10 minutes   |   Cook Time: 1 hr 30 min   |   Total: 1 hr 40 min

Ingredients

  • 1-1/2 cups water
  • 2-1/2 cups cooked bulgur
  • 1-1/2 cups evaporated milk
  • 1/3 cup sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon mace
  • 1/2 cup seedless raisins

Instructions

  1. Combine all ingredients in a baking dish.
  2. Cover and bake in moderate oven, 325°F (160°C), 45 minutes.
  3. Remove cover, stir, and continue baking uncovered about 45 minutes or until browned.
