Steamed Bulgur Fruit Pudding

A steamed loaf-style fruit pudding with bulgur and dried fruits.

Category: Dessert   |   Cuisine: International

Prep Time: 30 minutes   |   Cook Time: 2 hr   |   Total: 2 hr 30 min

Ingredients

  • 1-1/4 cups sifted all-purpose flour
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup butter or shortening
  • 1-1/4 cups sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1-1/2 cups cooked bulgur
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • 1/2 cup dried prunes (pitted), ground
  • 1/2 cup raisins, ground
  • 1/4 cup dried apricots, ground
  • 1 cup milk

Instructions

  1. Sift together flour, baking powder and salt.
  2. Cream butter and sugar until light and fluffy; add eggs and beat until thick and creamy.
  3. Stir in cooked bulgur and vanilla; add ground fruit and milk alternately with dry ingredients.
  4. Fill greased loaf pan or mould three-fourths full, cover tightly and steam for 2 hours. Serve with whipped cream.
