Scotch Broth with Bulgur Wheat

A traditional Scottish soup transformed with hearty bulgur wheat and lamb.

Category: Soup   |   Cuisine: Scottish

Prep Time: 15 minutes   |   Cook Time: 105 minutes   |   Total: 120 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs. lamb shoulder
  • 8 cups water
  • 1/2 cup bulgur wheat
  • 1 cup diced carrots
  • 1 cup diced turnip
  • 1 cup chopped leeks
  • 1/2 cup pearl barley
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Fresh parsley for garnish
  • 2 quarts chicken broth
  • 2 pounds lamb shank
  • 1 cup chopped parsley
  • 1/2 cup chopped onion
  • 1/2 cup chopped carrots
  • 1/2 cup chopped celery
  • 1/2 cup chopped green pepper (optional)
  • 1/2 cup chopped tomato
  • 1/2 cup chopped mint
  • 1/2 cup chopped leeks
  • 1/2 cup chopped bulghur wheat
  • 1/2 tsp. salt
  • 1/2 tsp. pepper

Instructions

  1. In a large pot, add lamb and water. Bring to a boil and skim off any foam.
  2. Reduce heat and simmer for 1 hour until lamb is tender.
  3. Remove lamb, shred meat, and return to pot.
  4. Add bulgur wheat, barley, carrots, turnip, and leeks.
  5. Simmer for 45 minutes until grains and vegetables are tender.
  6. Season with salt and pepper.
  7. Serve hot, garnished with fresh parsley.
  8. Boil lamb shank in chicken broth until tender. Remove meat and bones.
  9. Cut meat into small pieces and return to broth.
  10. Add all vegetables and herbs. Simmer for 15 minutes.
  11. Add bulghur wheat. Simmer for 15 minutes.
  12. Slowly stir in 8 cups of soup mixture, then add remaining soup.
  13. Continue cooking until thickened, about 3 minutes.
