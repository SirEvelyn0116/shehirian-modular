Bulgur Wheat Salad

Fresh herbs, lemon dressing, and hearty bulgur.

Category: Salad   |   Cuisine: Mediterranean

Prep Time: 15 minutes   |   Cook Time: 10 minutes   |   Total: 25 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup bulgur wheat
  • 1/2 cup chopped parsley
  • 1/4 cup chopped mint
  • 1/2 cup diced cucumber
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice

Instructions

  1. Soak bulgur in boiling water for 10 minutes until tender.
  2. Drain excess water and fluff with a fork.
  3. Mix with chopped parsley, mint, and diced cucumber.
  4. Drizzle with olive oil and lemon juice.
  5. Toss well and chill before serving.
