Bulgur Indian Pudding

A molasses-sweet bulgur pudding spiced with cinnamon and ginger.

Category: Dessert   |   Cuisine: International

Prep Time: 15 minutes   |   Cook Time: 1 hr 30 min   |   Total: 1 hr 45 min

Ingredients

  • 2-1/2 cups cooked bulgur
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1/4 teaspoon ginger
  • 1/8 teaspoon cloves
  • 1/3 cup sugar
  • 1/2 cup molasses
  • 3 tablespoons butter
  • 1/2 cup raisins (optional)
  • 1 large can evaporated milk
  • 1-1/4 cups water

Instructions

  1. Mix all ingredients in a 2-quart casserole.
  2. Cover and bake in a slow oven (325°F / 160°C) for 45 minutes.
  3. Remove cover and continue baking about 45 minutes until set.
  4. Serve warm with whipped cream or ice cream.
